Studio philosophy

A home that sounds
like its owner

For me, design is a careful way of turning a feeling into a room. It does not matter how many things a space holds if together they say nothing about the people who live there. Comfort comes from a whole idea, and from materials, textures and objects that agree with each other.

Each project is led by one architect from the first meeting to the handover, with the whole studio behind them.

Ilse Kavell,Founder
